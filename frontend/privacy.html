Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 · App Version: v1.0.0 · Package: com.haru.sora

1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy

Sora is an offline-capable, interactive creative fiction and character storytelling application built by Harshit Gupta. We believe that creative roleplay is personal, intimate, and sacred.

Sora operates on a Local-First Principle: your chat logs, created characters, story branches, character ties/bonds, and custom persona details are stored directly on your physical device via private app storage and sandboxed IndexedDB/LocalStorage. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal creative fiction data.

2. Summary of Data Practices

Category Collected? Purpose

3. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. Local Creative Fiction Content

Your conversations, customized lore notes, pinned Core Memories, user personas, and custom avatars reside in your local app storage. You maintain 100% ownership and control over this content.

B. AI Inference & Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)

When you send a message to a character:

  • The conversation context is transmitted over encrypted TLS (HTTPS) to the selected model provider (e.g., Sora Core, OpenRouter, Groq, or Gemini).
  • If you choose to use your own API keys (BYOK), your keys are stored strictly in client-side secure storage and are never logged on our servers.
  • We explicitly configure upstream API calls with zero-data-retention flags where supported.

C. Social Single Sign-On (Google & Facebook)

If you voluntarily choose to sign in with Google or Facebook SSO:

  • We collect your basic public profile information (Display Name, Email Address, and Avatar URL) solely to identify your account across app installations.
  • We do not request permissions to access your friends list, timeline, or contacts.

4. App Permissions & Hardware Usage

Sora requests only the minimal system permissions necessary to deliver roleplay immersion:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate with AI inference gateways and fetch community character updates.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used for subtle 8–15ms tactile feedback when pressing buttons, sending messages, or leveling up character ties.
  • Audio Output: Uses the device's native Speech Synthesis engine (TTS) to vocalize character dialogue on demand.
  • Photo Library / File Access: Only accessed when you explicitly tap to upload a custom character portrait, scene background, or import a JSON backup file.

5. Your Data Rights, Portability & Complete Deletion

You have absolute control over your information in Sora:

  • 1-Click Backup Export: Export all your characters, sessions, personas, and ties as an unencrypted, portable JSON file at any time from the Account Settings menu.
  • 1-Click Backup Restore: Import your backup on any new device or web browser.
  • Immediate Deletion: You can delete individual chats, characters, or purge the entire local cache by clicking "Clear All Data" in Account Settings or clearing the app storage via Android Settings.
  • Account Deletion: To delete your cloud account and associated email, tap "Delete Account" in the Account profile modal or email us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Sora is designed for creative writers, roleplayers, and interactive fiction enthusiasts. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Security & Anti-Modding Protection

Sora employs state cryptographic checksum validation and packaged asset verification to protect users against malicious tampered APKs distributed by unofficial third-party mod websites. All network communications are enforced via HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
